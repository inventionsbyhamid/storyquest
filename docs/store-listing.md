**Title:** StoryQuest

**Short description:** AI filmed family adventures you steer with your remote. Pick what happens next, or say your own idea.

**Long description:**

Tonight's story is yours to choose.

StoryQuest turns your TV into a story you make together. Every scene is a short film made by AI, with music and sound. When a scene ends, pick what happens next with your remote. Or press the microphone button, say your own idea, and watch it come to life a few seconds later.

New stories every day. Everyone starts from the same openings, but every family ends up somewhere different. At the end, see how rare your path was and share your whole story as one movie with a QR code.

Made for families. Choose Kids, Family or Grown ups and every story is written for that audience. Every idea and every scene is checked for safety before it is filmed.

* Fresh stories every day, free to watch and steer
* Say your own idea with the remote's microphone and see it filmed
* Seamless scenes that flow like one film
* Share your ending with a QR code
* Your first custom scene is free. Sparks film more scenes just for your family.
